Surgery, as a pentad

A sharp a priori for AI-assisted surgery: hemorrhage control, depth on curved tissue, and the organs nobody transplants. Hypotheses, not findings — built from slides, to be tested against the data.

I · Engine · MusicThe vessel exists whether or not anything sees it

The Engine is the patient: vessel, injury, flow. Prelingual, indifferent to the camera. Object permanence is the surgeon's carried root: beneath the blood, the injury is still there.

II · Sample · StatisticsA frame is a 2D sample of a 3D Engine

Next-token prediction in frames (t → t+1) is a Stage II operation. The error lives in the dimension the projection loses: depth. Hardware depth is a fix at the I→II edge (better transduction); algorithmic depth is a fix at II→III (better inference).

III · AdaptationSurgery is Stage III, literally

Insertions, deletions, translocations, permutations, combinations are the operative verbs. Simulation plus generative AI permutes scenes (vessel, site, flow rate) at scale.

DeletionResection, ligationRemoving a vessel or tissue layer from a scene

Risk: a generator trained on the distribution it feeds shares its own blind spots. Perfect sim depth is free, and no real sensor reproduces it.

IV · RegulationThe rootless voicing is the occluded vessel

A 3rd-and-7th shell fits both G7 and D♭7. A visible blood pattern likewise fits several injured vessels, and a wrong guess is costly. Two mechanisms hide under one word:

Resolution: the critical view of safety is a human consonance check. Does the model output a distribution over injury sites, or a point estimate?

V · Technology → I′Survivors, graveyard, institutions

Actions (cauterize, suction, pack, ligate) are embodiment. The data are the survivors: edited complication videos, and organs that were transplanted. Nothing is recorded for the discarded.
